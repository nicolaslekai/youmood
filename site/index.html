Three layers, all optional

The first layer works instantly and offline. The other two make it smarter.

1 · Rules

Word lists per category, English and German. Trump, MAGA, Bundesliga, breaking, sponsored. Zero latency, nothing leaves your browser.

2 · Claude on your Mac

Unclear titles are handed to the Claude CLI on your own subscription, straight from the browser. Cached for a week per video.

3 · API key

Or paste an Anthropic key and let Claude label directly from the browser. Pick the model yourself.

Install

youmood is its own browser for macOS (Apple silicon). Source on GitHub.

  1. Download youmood-0.2.0-arm64.dmg and drag youmood into Applications.
  2. First start: right-click youmood → Open (the build is not notarized yet).
  3. Type a search or a URL, click the youmood button, switch on what you want gone.

Still on Chrome? The original extension is here too: youmood-extension.zip, load unpacked via chrome://extensions.
